## Image-Domain Comparison

The presentation illustrates a qualitative comparison between detection on **Bangladesh-based aerial imagery** and **foreign imagery**:

- Slide 32 highlights stronger prediction on Bangladesh-based imagery and weaker prediction on foreign imagery for the team's model.
- Slide 33 illustrates the converse comparison for a model evaluated on foreign imagery.

These are qualitative observations presented in the original thesis, rather than independently established generalization claims.

## Geolocation and Mapping Demonstration

Slide 39 documents a mapping demonstration in which an unseen image is uploaded, its location is identified using geolocation information, and the result is displayed with Google Maps.

## Experimental Context

The study's research workflow includes:

1. Drone-based aerial-image collection in Bangladesh.
2. Preparation and manual annotation of a custom infrastructure dataset.
3. Model experimentation involving YOLOv5, YOLOv7, YOLOv8, and a custom approach.
4. Evaluation, prediction visualization, and qualitative comparison.
5. Exploration of urban-planning applications.
