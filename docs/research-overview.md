# Research Overview

## Research Question

How can drone-captured imagery and object-detection models support the identification of urban infrastructure in a Bangladesh-based setting?

## Study Context

The team collected aerial imagery in the Merul Badda area of Dhaka and developed a manually annotated dataset representing 11 object classes. The study examined YOLOv5, YOLOv7, YOLOv8, and a custom detection approach.

## Research Workflow

The research progressed from drone-image acquisition and image preparation to manual annotation, model experimentation, prediction visualization, and comparative analysis.

## Research Significance

The work investigates whether locally collected aerial imagery can support infrastructure detection and visual analysis for urban-planning applications. The original presentation also includes qualitative image-domain comparisons and a geolocation demonstration.
