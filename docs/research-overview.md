# Research Overview

## Study

**Enhancing Infrastructure Detection Using Drone Imagery: A Comparative Analysis Using Machine Learning Models with a Custom Bangladesh-Based Dataset for Improved Urban Planning**

Published in the *2024 13th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE)*.

**Publication DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ICECE64886.2024.11025116

## Research objective

The research examines infrastructure detection from drone-captured imagery in a Bangladesh-based urban-planning context. It combines local aerial-image acquisition and dataset annotation with comparative investigation of object-detection models.

## Dataset development

The research team collected aerial imagery using a drone and prepared a custom dataset. Images were annotated manually across 11 infrastructure classes.

## Model comparison

The study includes YOLOv5, YOLOv7, YOLOv8, and a custom model in its comparative research scope.

## Applications

The study relates computer-vision-based infrastructure identification to analysis of urban environments and planning.
