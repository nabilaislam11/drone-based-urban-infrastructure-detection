# Research Workflow

The research combines drone-based image acquisition, dataset development, object detection, and comparative analysis to investigate infrastructure recognition in Bangladesh.

## 1. Aerial Image Acquisition

The research team acquired aerial imagery using a drone to capture infrastructure and urban environments in Bangladesh.

## 2. Dataset Preparation and Annotation

Captured images were organized into a custom dataset and manually annotated across **11 infrastructure classes**. This dataset supports the comparative object-detection study.

## 3. Object-Detection Experiments

The study investigated the following approaches:

- **YOLOv5**
- **YOLOv7**
- **YOLOv8**
- **Custom model**

The experimental scope centers on recognizing infrastructure in aerial imagery.

## 4. Results Analysis

Experimental results were analyzed and visualized to support comparison of the detection approaches and their application to urban infrastructure identification.

## 5. Research Dissemination

The study was published in the proceedings of the *2024 13th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE)*.

[Publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICECE64886.2024.11025116)
